## SWOT
*Strength Weaknesses Opportunities Threats*

| -------- | Perks         | Handicaps  |
| -------- | ------------- | ---------- |
| Internal | Strength      | Weaknesses |
| Market   | Opportunities | Threats    |
test
### Strength
Resources or factors allowing the studied company to perform better than the concurrence

>[!tip] Examples
> - A strong notoriety
> - A Facebook page with >1 MIL followers
> - An accessible price

### Weaknesses
Internal negative traits of the company that need a substantial upgrade

>[!tip] Examples
>- Lowering website visits
>- Low notoriety
>- A product with limited distribution
### Opportunities
external factors/situations that the company could take advantage of / leverage 

>[!tip] Examples
>- A change in legislation that favors a line of product in which the company could specialize
>- A new trend that ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
>- A new material that could allow the company to lower production cost -> sell at competitive prices

### Threats
External transformation of the environment/market that could degrade the company's position

>[!tip] Examples
>- Opposites of opportunities...


### Crossing Strategies

**SO** (Strength + Opportunities) -> Associate interesting Strength with growing opportunities.
- Use company's strength to take advantage of exterior opportunities

**WO** (Weakness + Opportunities) -> upgrade or work on a weakness in the company to leverage/take advantage of a growing opportunity.
Reduces company weaknesses, and also takes advantage of opportunity so... *its good*

## Strategical Analysis Tools

### Micro Environment
To study the micro environment, The 5 [[Marketing Canvas.canvas|Porter Forces]]:
- threatening new developing companies
- threats on products or substitution products
-  Concurrence between rivals
- Customer negotiating capabilities
- Supplier negotiation capabilities

>[!tip] Customer and Supplier negotiation capabilities
>This is simply the ability of clients or suppliers to negotiate based on how many other companies are offering the same service or products.
>
>If there is no concurrence, then the client cannot negotiate, if he doesn't want to stay with you, he has nowhere else to go.
>
>Same principle applies to the supplier, if the supplier has many clients, he has the power to negotiate.

### Macro Environment

The [[Marketing Canvas.canvas|Pestel Method]] is used to study the Macro Environment
