The first step to starting a digital communication campaign is to be able to answer the following questions:

- Why be present on the web?
- To answer what needs?
- Is our target audience present?
- Why do our targets use the web?
- Their long/short term motivations

***

## Define Objectives and Goals

Answering the question above requires to first have an **Audit and Diagnostic** phase.
The digital communication is **only** an offspring of the larger strategic plan. You need to define goals and objectives closely linked to the communication media you are using. 
#### Example Objectives

- Modernize the brand's image
- Start a new service
- Create a community

***

## User centrism : studying the user behavior

Basically show the right information at the right time with a creative aspect that closely relates to the targeted audience/user 

*Make it nice and useful ONLY for your target audience...*

## Passer a l'action

- The digital means that most relate to the enterprise's needs (*What platforms*)

# Making a short form content video

## Objective 1 : First Step

### Main Objective

First figure out the main objective of the video. What is its goal towards the user?
*Examples:*
- Promote or change brand identity
- Attract more users, more interactions.
- become the opinion leader in a particular niche

### Content Type

Figure out what type of content you want to use to promote the main objective
- Tutorial
- Ad spot
- viral video (undefinable, its pure buzz)
- client/user interview
- backstage/behind the scene

### Project duration

- plan ahead the creation time needed for each separate task using a **Gantt**(*look it up idc*) diagram
- Plan the required video time, find the perfect length for the video.:
	- Tutorial : **1-2min**
	- Ad Spot : **15-30s**
	- Viral Video : **random**
	- Client interview : **2-3min**
	- backstage/behind the scenes : **1-2min**

### Scenario and storyboard

Writing of the scenario, comprised of:
- Dialogues
- off-screen voices
- movements

Think of it as a screenplay, except with a different dialect. Try to avoid profanity *or don't gofy*
