
You are a dungeon crawler. You go into dungeons, and clear them. Find in each dungeons, loot, which can be used to craft new gear, or sold for cash to buy other useful items.

## Story

There is a dungeon infestation in a faraway fantasy land, the issue is that this leads to monsters roaming the land. Dungeons act as nest for monsters to reproduce, and you have been called to clear them out so the villagers can keep on living.

The villagers don't know that you lied on your resume, you have nothing on you, and you're terrified, but you're not backing out.
## Game Details

Top down, 2d. Combat is realtime, variety of monsters each with their own loottables.
Variety of dungeons, each containing specific monsters with a specific spawn rate.

E.g. In a cave dungeon, spider has 50% chance of spawning, and has a 20% chance of dropping spider eye, which can be used to craft a special sword or sold for cash.

## How to win

For now, you have 5 days, after the 5 days, your score is equal to how many dungeons you were able to clear, and all your player stats (overall damage, overall health, and so on.)
