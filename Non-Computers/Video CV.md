
**Main Idea: What happens inside my mind / Creative Process**

Concepts to transmit:
- All over the place
- Not giving up
- Always learning new things
- Passion for code and computers

*A video shot fully at home, where you see the process from me getting a mail, to me actually doing it. Explores concept of giving up, coming back, obsession, and passion*

The idea is that, I receive a mail asking me to do something, I get on it, work on it, it doesn't work, I give up, watch tv, get pissed I gave up, get back to it, work until 3am, it still doesn't work, wake up the next morning, it works, I get a call camera stays fixed on screen which shows in ascii contact me flashing (the code.)

The entire thing has me as a narrator, describing what is happening.
