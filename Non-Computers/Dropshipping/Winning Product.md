Winning product refers to a product which you will be able to make important profit on.

Some basic rules of thumb to stay away from:
- Stay away from electronics *hard to guarantee quality*
- Stay away from comestible and ingestible *regulation and law issues*
- Stay away from glassware *obvious transportation risks*


# Selling price

For a product bought at around 10$ a piece, sell it for a *bare* minimum of **40$** and a maximum of **$80**. This is to ensure you have enough money to invest into ads

**ADS ARE YOUR MAIN SELLING POINT**

Your goal is to ad, ad , AD, **AD**. Feed ads to the clients.

# Ads and Promotion

To promote your product start small, tryout a bunch of different ads until one sticks, then develop upon it.

## Step 1 : Tryout
### TikTok

TikTok ads will target mainly a younger audience, teens and young adults. 
Short form video content, you need to capture the attention within the first 3 seconds *hook*.

### Facebook

Facebook will target an older audience, adults and old people.
Simple photos are sufficient here, promote your product with... *product photos*, and redirect to your website.

## Step 2: Expand

Once your target audience has been found, and you've created a sufficient amount of views on social media ads. Move on to google ads. You tube ads, influencers, personality. Feed your product to the potential clients, they need to see it everywhere, they need to be submerged in it.

*it goes without saying, contact influencers and personalities in your niche...*

# Creating the brand

Initially, sell one or a few products of a fixed niche. **stay in the niche**. dropship the products directly with no brand. create a simple logo and brand name + identity for the website to follow solely.

As you expand, contact the manufacturer to create custom product, with your brand name, with illustrations, ideas. IDC, anything to sell more, make it custom and unique.
